What is collected

This site has no accounts and no forms. The only thing it records is usage stats, through Google Analytics: which page was viewed, the type of device and browser, and an approximate location that Google works out from your IP address. The page address is sent without anything after a question mark.

What it is never used for

Usage stats are never sold, never used for ads and never linked to a name or email. Google signals and ad personalisation are switched off. No Google Ads account is connected.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets its own cookies so a return visit counts as the same visitor. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland it sets no cookies and Google gets an anonymous page count only.

How long it is kept

Two months, the shortest period Google Analytics allows.

Turning it off

Switch it off here or at the foot of the home page. Once off, Google's script is not loaded at all and its cookies are removed. It stays off in this browser. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, usage stats start switched off.

Who else sees a visit

Cloudflare hosts the site and handles each request, which means it processes your IP address to deliver the page and protect it from abuse.
